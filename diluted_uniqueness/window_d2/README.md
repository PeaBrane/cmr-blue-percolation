# d = 2: the exact local-ratio window g_2 = 2g*

This directory holds the computer-assisted part of the two-dimensional window
theorem of the diluted-model manuscript: for `d = 2` the conclusions of the
main theorem hold for every `p < 1/2 + g*`, with

    g* = 1059411733898907047504026797 / 90604240507898863756433027512000
       in [1.1692738970716e-5, 1.1692738970717e-5].

In the normalisation `p < p_c + g_2/2` of the main theorem this is
`g_2 = 2g*` in `[2.3385477941433e-5, 2.3385477941434e-5]`, about `10^295.84`
times the surgery constant `g_2 = 2^-988/1130`. The classical-window
threshold becomes `beta_*(2) = (1/2) ln(1/(2g*))` in
`[5.3316976645232, 5.3316976645233]` (`T_* ≈ 0.18756`).

The proof (manuscript) reduces the window to finitely many exact
inequalities between integer counts on small boxes. The spacing-3 sparse
diminishment puts every candidate plaquette's star in its own `4 x 4` cell,
so conditioning on the outside reduces pivotality to a finite problem indexed
by a non-crossing boundary partition. The curve is split in two paths:

- **Path 2** (the diamond activation grows, only star edges move): the cell
  inequality **(C2)** at `u = 0` and `u = 1/2` for every cell boundary
  condition (`3,879,876` of them);
- **Path 1** (Bernoulli, `s = 0`): grid edges are traded against star edges
  through a Farkas split `c_side + c_int = (g*, g*, g* - a_P, g* - a_T)` over
  two box families with an exact weighted coverage identity; the inequalities
  **(C1-side)**, **(C1-o)** (side box containing the origin) and
  **(C1-int)** hold for every boundary condition of those boxes; boxes near
  `S_L` are treated separately.

**(C0)** collects the relations between the frozen constants: `a_T = (9/5) a_P`,
`eta = 1/25000 >= max(g*, a_P, a_T)`, the Farkas split, `c_int` supported on
`{Gc, T}`, and the signs used for the special boxes. All arithmetic is exact
(integers and `fractions.Fraction`); no rounding enters any inequality.

## Records

A boundary condition is `beta = (pi, X, Y)`: `pi` a non-crossing partition of
the box's boundary vertices (in cyclic order, written as a restricted growth
string) and two distinct blocks `X < Y` (in the origin box `Y` is the
origin's own block). A record holds nine exact integers
`D, N0[Gc], N0[Gm], N0[P], N0[T], N1[Gc], N1[Gm], N1[P], N1[T]`: `D` counts
inside configurations in which the candidate plaquette is s-pivotal, and
`Na[c]` is the class-`c`-weighted number of pairs `(f, z)` with `f`
(+)-pivotal for `X <-> Y` at candidate activation `a`. Path-1 boxes have no
candidate (`D = 0`, `N1 = 0`). The exact definition, the byte layout of the
engine dumps and of the canonical streams are in
[lrm_common.py](lrm_common.py).

| family | box | edges | boundary positions | non-crossing partitions | records |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `side` | side box of a grid segment | 17 | 10 | 16,796 | 218,790 |
| `sideo` | side box containing the origin | 17 | 10 + origin | 16,796 | 92,378 |
| `int` | intersection box `c + [-1,1]^2` | 12 | 8 | 1,430 | 12,012 |
| `cell` (all) | cell `3k + [0,3]^2` with its candidate | 24 | 12 | 208,012 | 3,879,876 |
| `cell_reps` | one partition per `D4` orbit | 24 | 12 | 26,584 orbits | 496,272 |

The counts of a cell record are invariant under the eight symmetries of the
cell (a hand-proved lemma of the manuscript), so (C2) on the reduced list
`cell_reps` implies (C2) for all `3,879,876` cell boundary conditions once the
list is a complete system of orbit representatives. The full reproduction
also recomputes all partitions and checks this invariance record by record.

## Default light check

```sh
python diluted_uniqueness/window_d2/check_window_d2.py
```

Standard library only; about 35–40 s with CPython 3.13.5 (Apple Silicon or
x86-64 Linux), peak memory about 150 MB (Linux) to 200 MB (macOS). It decides:

1. **Constants.** (C0) on the frozen inputs; `g*` is the displayed rational;
   the enclosures of `g*`, `g_2 = 2g*`, `beta_*(2)`, `1/beta_*(2)` and of the
   gain `log10(g_2 / (2^-988/1130))` in `[295.8396, 295.8397]`; every decimal
   of the constants table.
2. **Geometry.** The pattern facts and the weighted coverage identity of both
   Path-1 families on all 1,250 edges of `[-12,12]^2`; the four spec files are
   regenerated and equal the committed ones.
3. **Special boxes.** The `S_L`-centred box (25 data; maximum
   `N_Gc/N_T = 3/4`; the per-record inequality with exponent 4) and the box
   census for `L = 9, 12, 15, 30`.
4. **Path 1.** Each committed Path-1 stream has the recorded digest, is
   strictly sorted and complete (every non-crossing partition, with exactly
   its admissible block pairs); (C1-side), (C1-o) and (C1-int) hold on every
   record, exactly; the maximal ratios (positive part)/(negative part) are
   `0.651213`, `0.978415`, `0.993495`.
5. **Path 2.** The reduced cell list has the recorded digest and is a complete
   system of `D4`-orbit representatives (the 26,584 orbits of the 208,012
   non-crossing partitions; the one-block orbit has no block pair), covering
   `3,879,876` boundary conditions. (C2) holds at `u = 0` and `u = 1/2` on all
   `496,272` records, exactly, with maxima `0.993503` and `0.993729` (margin
   `>= 6.2e-3`); no record has `D = 0` and a pivotal star edge. The binding
   value is attained by 20 boundary conditions (4 reduced records; `D` = 276,
   552, 828 for 8, 8, 4 of them), and 69,292 cell boundary conditions have a
   pivotal grid edge and `D = 0`.
6. **Record correctness, in part.** The independent Python engine recomputes
   the whole intersection family (digest equal) and every record of the side
   and origin partitions in a deterministic subset (every 32nd partition plus
   those of the tightest records: 533 and 526 partitions, 6,867 and 2,898
   records), all equal.

**Not decided by the default run:** that the committed cell records and the
side and origin records outside that subset are the counts that their
definition prescribes. Given the records, every inequality is decided. The
full reproduction decides the rest.

Optional: `python check_window_d2.py --with-cc` also compiles
`engine/jbrute.c` (C compiler `CC`, default `cc`) and brute-forces the four
binding reduced cell records and 8 fixed-seed random ones (about 2 s each),
which decides those 12 cell records independently of the engine.

The run ends with a JSON summary equal to
[../../expected/diluted_window_d2.json](../../expected/diluted_window_d2.json).

## Full reproduction

```sh
python diluted_uniqueness/window_d2/reproduce_window_d2.py --threads 6 --workdir /path/to/scratch
```

Needs a C compiler with POSIX threads (`CC`, default `cc`) and Python 3.10+
(standard library). It writes about 400 MB of engine dumps to the work
directory (default `window_d2/build/`, which should not be committed) and:

1. regenerates the spec files and compiles `engine/lrm.c`;
2. recomputes every family from scratch: the three Path-1 families on all
   non-crossing partitions, the reduced cell list, and the cell family on all
   208,012 partitions;
3. puts each dump in canonical form and checks its SHA-256 against the table
   above (for the committed families this equals the committed file);
4. checks (C1) on every regenerated Path-1 record and (C2) on all 3,879,876
   cell records exactly, completeness of the all-partition family, and that
   every record equals the record of its `D4` image in the reduced list;
5. compiles `engine/jbrute.c` and `engine/jbrute1.c` and compares them with
   sampled records: the binding and tightest records of every inequality
   and fixed-seed random ones.

Reference run (clean copy of the repository, x86-64 Linux, gcc 13.3.0,
CPython 3.13.5, 6 threads, shared machine): 9 min 49 s wall, 49 CPU-minutes
(engine: 455 s for all 208,012 cell partitions, 94 s for the reduced list,
under 10 s for the Path-1 families), peak memory 1.3 GB; the 141 brute-force
samples (52 cell, 32 side, 25 origin, 32 intersection) all agree. Elapsed
times in the JSON are not reproducibility targets. Its summary is
[expected_outputs/reproduce_window_d2.json](expected_outputs/reproduce_window_d2.json).

## Relation to the proof

The manuscript proves the reduction of the infinite system to these boxes
(planarity gives non-crossing boundary partitions), the two-path curve, the
Farkas split and the coverage identity, the treatment of the boxes near the
origin and near `S_L`, and the `D4` invariance of the cell counts. The
programs certify the finite inequalities (C0)–(C2) on the explicit records.
Three implementations of the records agree: the engine `engine/lrm.c`
(general transition keys, all partitions), the literal brute force
`engine/jbrute*.c` (one boundary condition at a time, no tables), and, for the
Path-1 boxes, the Python engine in `lrm_common.py`. The research computation
additionally agreed with the program that first produced the certificate and
with two further independent record computations; those programs are not
included here. The code of `engine/*.c`, `geom.py` and `sl_box.py` is that of
the audited research version; only header comments and docstrings were
edited.

Not claimed: any Monte Carlo estimate of the true window, and near-sharpness
of `g*`.
